A few concepts, made simple.

01 /

Payoff

An organization’s utility score for an outcome. Higher means better within this model.

02 /

Nash equilibrium

A decision pair where neither player can earn more by changing its own decision alone.

03 /

Dominant strategy

A choice that earns more against every possible choice of the other player.

04 /

Collective benefit

The sum of both scores. Maximizing this total does not always produce an equilibrium.
